## 一、七场景真实冒烟（脚本 `backend/scripts/smoke_scenes_real.py`）

每个场景创建会话 + 发一条代表性文字回答，记录真实评估/教练动作/回复：

| 场景 | 用户回答 | 评估 | 动作 | 教练回复 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 英文面试 | I have three years of experience in software development. | incomplete | follow_up | That's great! What kind of software projects did you work on? |
| 酒店入住 | I have a reservation under my name. | incomplete | hint | Could you please tell me your name? For example, 'My name is Alex.' |
| 会议中表达意见 | I think we should launch the smaller version first. | incomplete | follow_up | That's a clear opinion about product launch timing. Why do you think starting with the smaller version is the best choice? |
| 认识新同事 | I am a designer on the product team. | independent | follow_up | Nice to meet you! What kind of design do you focus on? |
| 餐厅点餐 | I would like the grilled chicken, please. | independent | follow_up | Great choice! Would you like that with fries or a side salad? |
| 认识新朋友 | I am a teacher and I like hiking. | independent | follow_up | That's great! What's your favorite hiking spot? |
| 聊兴趣爱好 | I like painting in my free time. | independent | follow_up | That sounds relaxing! What kind of things do you like to paint? |

**结论**：七场景教练追问均贴合各自场景，无英文面试残留。评估结果与 2026-09-22 相比整体偏严（评估器已收紧，属预期），`会议中表达意见` 等简短表达从 independent 降为 incomplete。

**发现 1 处待判**：酒店入住对「I have a reservation under my name.」给了 `hint`（而非追问姓名），且示例「For example, 'My name is Alex.'」中的 Alex 非场景配置内容（配置里是 Li，见 `scenarios.py` help_materials），为模型自发生成的占位名。性质轻于 v5 之前的「照抄示例当事实」，但「对已含姓名意图的回答给答案级 hint」值得在 P1 REQ-34 对话节奏质检时一并校准。不阻断本轮收口。

## 三、时延 / 失败率 / 费用（脚本 `measure_latency.py --turns 20`）

> 方法同 2026-09-23：每回合 = 独立评估 LLM + 教练 LLM + TTS，直接测百炼服务，未含 HTTP/SSE 网络开销与真实 ASR。

| 段 | P50 | P95 | 均值 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 评估 LLM（qwen-plus） | 0.63s | 0.85s | 0.67s |
| 教练 LLM（qwen-plus） | 1.28s | 1.58s | 1.33s |
| TTS（cosyvoice-v2） | 2.76s | 3.46s | 2.87s |
| 单回合合计(估) | 5.02s | 5.50s | 4.87s |

- **失败率**：1/20（5%），TTS websocket 建连超时（5s 未建立）。本脚本为**裸调无重试**；后端 `DashScopeTTSProvider` 已带有限重试，实际用户路径会重试 1 次。
- **费用**：LLM 24,419 in / 1,183 out tokens ≈ ¥0.022；TTS 1,261 字符 ≈ ¥2.522；合计 ¥2.544（20 回合），**单回合约 ¥0.127**（与上轮 ¥0.13 一致）。

**结论**：与 2026-09-23 基线（单回合 P50 4.50s / P95 5.55s / ¥0.13）相比**无回退**（P50 略升至 5.02s 属噪声）。累计采样 50+20=70 回合，仍不足 PRD 13.1 的 200 回合大样本；扩量可后续脚本再跑或纳入真实内测一并采。TTS 仍是时延与失败率大头（99% 成本）。
